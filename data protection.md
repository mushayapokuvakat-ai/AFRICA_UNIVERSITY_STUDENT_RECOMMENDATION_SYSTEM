# Data Retention and Student Data Protection Notice

**Student Portfolio System, Africa University**
**Last updated:** 14 November 2026

This notice explains what student data the Student Portfolio System holds, why we hold it, who can see it, how long we keep it, and how we protect it.

## 1. What data we hold

- **Account data:** name, student ID, email address, programme, and role.
- **Portfolio data:** activities, achievements, skills, reflections, and attachment (work placement) records.
- **Evidence files:** documents, images, and certificates uploaded to support portfolio entries.
- **Review data:** supervisor feedback, approvals, and verification requests and outcomes.
- **Security data:** login times and audit logs of role and access changes.

## 2. Why we hold it

- To let students build and present a verified record of their achievements.
- To support attachment supervision, career advising, and departmental review.
- To let staff and external verifiers confirm activities.
- To keep the system secure and accountable.

We do not sell student data or use it for advertising.

## 3. Who can see it

| Role | Access |
|---|---|
| Student | Their own data |
| Attachment supervisor | Data of students assigned to them |
| Head of Department (HOD) | Students in their department |
| Career advisor | Portfolio data needed for advising |
| Administrator | Account and audit data, for system management |
| External verifier | Only the specific activity they are asked to verify |

Access is controlled by role-based permissions.

## 4. How long we keep it

| Data | Retention period |
|---|---|
| Account and portfolio data | While the student is enrolled, plus 2 years after graduation or withdrawal |
| Evidence files | Same as portfolio data |
| Verification records | 3 years after the verification |
| Login and audit logs | 12 months |
| Deleted accounts | Removed from active systems within 30 days; backups are cleared within 90 days |

After these periods, data is deleted or anonymised.

## 5. How we protect it

- Passwords are stored hashed, never in plain text.
- Authentication uses signed tokens (JWT) with expiry.
- Role-based access control limits who can read or change data.
- Connections are encrypted with HTTPS.
- Secrets and credentials are kept in environment variables, not in the code.
- Role and access changes are recorded in an audit log.

## 6. Your rights

Students can request to:

- **Access** the data we hold about them.
- **Correct** inaccurate data.
- **Delete** their data, subject to the retention periods above and any legal obligations.
- **Export** a copy of their portfolio.
- **Object** to a particular use of their data.

To make a request, contact us using the details in section 8. We aim to respond within 30 days.

## 7. Third parties

We use service providers to run the system, such as:

- A hosting provider for the server and database.
- An email service for notifications and verifier emails.

These providers process data only to deliver their service. We share data with external verifiers only as described in section 3.
